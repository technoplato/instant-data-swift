add-query met the socket the system had aborted (POSIX 53) before the receiver took the death, the connection still
read as open, and the read gave up with the send failure and dropped its registration, so the reconnect that followed
sent nothing for it. Observers keep their registration for the reconnect; upstream Reactor.js leaves every queryOnce's
add-query to _flushPendingMessages on the next init-ok and rejects it only at QUERY_ONCE_TIMEOUT.
