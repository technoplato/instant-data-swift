# What a phone's local save writes, page by page, and what writes at launch (#1510), 2026-10-31

## Bottom line

**A local save on build 208 (1.9.56) writes 24.4 pages (98 KB) to the write-ahead log across 17 b-trees; the outbox's
own bookkeeping is most of it, the save's facts 6 pages.** A server confirm writes 35.5 pages, 12.9 of them live-query
ownership rows. Of the "7 GB launch catch-up" on 10-31, about 5.9 GB is Core ML recompiling the on-device speech models
after each install (#1429), not this library; the library's own catch-up after a launch reached 1.07 GB in one minute.
Measured on the phone's own files and with Apple's File Activity; the numbers below say how.

## How it was measured

- **The write-ahead log, frame by frame.** Two USB pulls of the store and its log, 13:21 and 13:32 EDT, build 208
  recording (private, never in a repository; read on a clone that SQLite never opened, so no checkpoint touched it).
  A frame is valid while its salts match the log header's and its cumulative checksum holds; each frame's page is named
  by `dbstat` on a clone with the log applied; a page `dbstat` no longer places was freed after the frame (an outbox
  body's overflow, or the free list's trunk). A save is told from a server confirm by its body leaf gaining a cell
  (insert) or losing one (delete). The 13:32 log held 308 commits and 3,642 frames (15.0 MB).
- **Apple's File Activity** (`device-lab.py capture disk`, Xcode 27 beta's `xctrace`, attached to the app, 13:27:24
  for 60 s while recording): every write syscall with its file descriptor, size and symbolicated backtrace; a SQLite
  write is named by the store call in its backtrace.
- **The energy ledger's minute rows** (`disk.writers`, `disk.unattributedLogicalBytes`, from `proc_pid_rusage`'s
  `ri_logical_writes`), matched minute by minute to the Mac collector's `onthisdevice.models.launch` lines.

## What one local write costs, by transaction (13:32 log)

| Transaction | Commits | Pages each | KB each | Where the pages go |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Save | 81 | 24.4 (median 23) | 98 | facts 5.93, write keys' lookup index 3.38, the body's overflow 2.70, the body 2.06, write keys 1.90, page 1 1.00 (one byte changed: the free-page count), `instant_sync_metadata` 1.00 (6 bytes: two revision counters), effect entities and their index 2.00, the outbox row 1.00 and three of its indexes 3.00 |
| Server apply and confirm | 26 | 35.5 (median 29) | 143 | live-query ownership 12.9 (`instant_live_query_triples` 4.35, its identity index 7.50, results 1.00), the confirmed write keys' deletes 8.04, outbox row and body 5.38, facts 1.08 |
| Acceptance | 84 | 5.0 | 20 | the watermark index 1.98, the outbox row, its delivery index, `instant_sync_metadata` (3 bytes) |
| Claim | 80 | 2.0 | 8 | the outbox row and its delivery index |
| Stored file records | 37 | 4.5 | 18 | `instant_files` and its two indexes |

A page changed by a few bytes is written whole: page 1 for one byte, the metadata page for 3-7, an outbox index leaf
for 50-150. The 13:21 log (180 commits) gives the same shape: a save 24.8 pages.

Bodies (89 new in the 13:32 log, median sizes): audio-segment records 6.9 KB (14 operations: the new segment's 9
fields and the stream's 4, #1253's partial write), transcript segments 11.8 KB (22 operations), recording updates
57 KB (16 operations and a 37 KB rollback). Every operation carries its transaction id and time again; a body past
about 1 KB spills into overflow pages in this `WITHOUT ROWID` table, each allocated from the free list (page 1 and the
trunk rewritten) at the save and freed at the confirm.

### By kind of save (13:32 log, a save's kind read from its body's operations)

| Kind | Saves | Pages | KB | Body KB | Operations | Largest b-trees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transcript segment | 7 | 32.6 | 131 | 17.9 | 50.4 | facts 9.71, write keys' index 4.14, body 3.14 + overflow 3.14, write keys 2.86 |
| Recording update | 7 | 29.7 | 120 | 40.5 | 12.6 | facts 6.86, overflow 6.71, body 4.00 |
| Audio-segment record | 37 | 25.1 | 101 | 6.8 | 14.0 | facts 6.32, write keys' index 4.81, overflow 2.14 |
| Timeline event (`recordingRuntimeEvents`) | 17 | 22.3 | 90 | 9.4 | 10.0 | facts 5.41, overflow 2.65 |
| Other | 13 | 17.9 | 72 | 6.9 | 12.5 | facts 2.92, overflow 2.15 |

Every kind pays the same 8 pages of bookkeeping: page 1, `instant_sync_metadata`, effect entities and their index, the
outbox row and three of its indexes. One transcript segment save, decoded: 22 operations, `requireEntityExists` on the
recording, `recordings/id` again, all 19 of the segment's fields (#1255: a line's write restates every field), a delete
of the line's live row, and a 21-operation rollback; its values come to about 1.3 KB and its stored body to 11.7 KB.

### What a save under 20 KB takes (criterion 1 of #1510)

20 KB is 5 pages. Measured floor of each lever, per save, from the table above; none is decided here.

| Lever | Pages a save | Pages a confirm | Owner, and what it touches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A line's write sends only what changed (#1255, as #1253 did for the stream row) | facts, keys, body: about -60% for segment saves | the echo's restatement | Scribe's write coordinator; offline, a partial write is not superseded by the next one, so the outbox would keep one row per interim unless supersession merges a partial write into its pending tail (library) |
| A compact stored body (no repeated transaction id and time, no type wrappers; the old encoding still decodes) | overflow and free-list churn: -2 to -4 | -2 to -4 | the outbox's body and its receipt (perf-send-1492's region, #1497) |
| Write keys and effect entities as one b-tree each (keyed for their lookups, no second index) | -3 | -5 | the outbox's claim, supersession and server-apply reads (about 10 statements) |
| Revision counters out of the per-write path (#1254) | -1 | -1 (and -1 an acceptance) | other connections and the query cache's stored revisions (`PRAGMA data_version`, a per-launch epoch; perf-read-1498's region) |
| The optimistic overlay kept out of `instant_triples` until the server's answer (upstream Instant overlays pending writes at read time) | the facts: -6 to -10 | the confirm's rewrite | an architecture decision (ADR 0001's materialization), the largest and the riskiest |

Measured since (13:32 log against the pulled file's free pages): a save allocates 0.65 new pages and rewrites 23.7 pages
that already belong to a b-tree, about one per b-tree it touches; splits are not the cost, the number of b-trees is.
Two more levers follow from that:

| Lever | Pages a save | Pages a confirm | Owner, and what it touches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Write keys and effect entities in `TEMP` tables (rebuilt from the durable bodies at open, refreshed when `PRAGMA data_version` says another connection wrote; #1504 keeps `TEMP` in memory) | -7.3 (four b-trees) | -10 | the same SQL reads them; a second process sharing the outbox is the case to prove |
| The stored body's version 2 with values kept byte-identical to `value_json`, so the claim's exact projected sizes stay exact (amendment 1) | -1 to -1.5 (a segment body 11.7 -> about 6.7 KB, still past 1 KB) | -0.5 | encode and decode only |

The first two (#1255, the compact body) cut bytes no reader needs; the `TEMP` tables cut the most pages with no change
to what is durable. Under 5 pages takes those three and the facts written once (the fifth lever above) or bodies that
never spill.

## Apple's File Activity, 60 s while recording (13:27)

27.0 MB written by write syscalls. SQLite 17.5 MB: the log 14.0 MB in 3,404 frames of 4,120 bytes (saveLocalMutation
6.48 MB, commitServerApplyPlan 5.46, acceptOutboxMutation 1.28, claimAutomaticOutboxDeliveryWindow 0.51,
writeStoredFileRecord 0.28) and one checkpoint, 837 pages (3.43 MB) into the database file in a burst Apple flags ("557
physical writes within 1 second"), on a save's call path. The rest is the app's: the recording's WAV 5.8 MB, the
diagnostics spool's compaction 1.29 MB, the InstantDB log file 1.0 MB, compressed-audio segments 0.37 MB, and three
writes with no app frame 0.77 MB.

## The 10-31 launch catch-up

The ledger's minutes not recording on 10-31 add to about 7 GB in 17 minutes. Five of them carry 1.17-1.21 GB each that
no writer names, and each is the minute of a Mac-collector line "#1057 On This iPhone's models compile again once:
container changed since Core ML compiled them" (05:32:35 build 202, 05:43:00 203, 06:41:21 204, 09:43:10 207 EDT; each
minute also read about 1 GB): Core ML after an install, #1429, about 5.9 GB. The library's own share is the catch-up
that follows: 10:43:24Z 1,073 MB (checkpoint 441, commitServerApplyPlan 371, released live-query chunks 75, their
prune 27), 13:43:48Z 505 MB (checkpoint 219, released chunks 188, prune 49), 04:47:36Z 710 MB (checkpoint 268, server
apply 245).

## What the library measures from the release after 1.9.56 (#1510)

`InstantSQLiteWriteRecorder`: every page the log takes and every page a checkpoint copies, by b-tree (table, index,
overflow, page 1, the free list), by write statement (runs, rows, its pages, the b-trees it writes) and by store call,
over time, read once a minute like the store's other meters. This document's tables, live on the phone.
