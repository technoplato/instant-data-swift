## 1.5.4 — Break dual-write diagnostic feedback thrash

### Problem

Scribe dual-writes Instant library diagnostics into Instant `debugLogs`. Routine
outbox/query/transport events at `.info`/`.notice` re-entered the outbox as
`debug-log-batch` mutations (~700 ops each), HOL-blocked the step budget, and
drove **multi-GB idle** physical footprint on iPad (field 2026-08-05: ~2.7–3.8 GB
sitting on the home screen; cold open 122 MB → multi‑GB in ~60 s).

### Library

High-frequency routine diagnostics demoted to **`.debug`**:
`outbox.flush.started/finished`, `outbox.mutation.send`, `outbox.mutation.transact-ok`,
`query-once.started/completed`, `transaction.started`, `transaction.optimistic-commit`,
`websocket.message-sent`.

Loud signals (HOL wait notice, reclaim warning, failures) unchanged.

Regression: `InstantDiagnosticFeedbackLoopTests`.

### Host (Scribe)

Also land InstantDBLogger bridge filter (chatter categories below notice) and
batch size 8 so log mutations fit under 256 in-flight steps.

Pin Scribe `exact: "1.5.4"`.
