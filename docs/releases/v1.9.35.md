# Instant Swift Data 1.9.35

Status: published under the maintainer's standing authorization for fixes, typed on 2026-10-15: "Fix things when
they're broken. Don't ask me permission." and the instruction of 2026-10-23 to land every green step on main. The last
code commit is `f83e9448`.

## Release focus

**An upload, a read by id, a delete and an eviction read no other file's record while nothing observes the files**
(#1072; red `347af4f6`, green `f83e9448`).

Each of them read and decoded every file record the device keeps, only to hand the list to observations of the
device's files (`observeStoredFiles()` offline or local-only), whether one was open or not; the upload did it holding the
operation gate. The consuming app opens none and uploads about eleven times a minute while recording (a 60 s audio
chunk and ten 6 s media segments), and the records grow with everything the device ever uploaded (4,264 on one phone,
about 3 MB of JSON). One record that did not decode also failed every upload after its work was done, and the failed
upload deleted the file storage had just taken.

The records are now read and published only while an observation is open, after the change is written as before, and
the upload still publishes under the gate so uploads publish in the order they saved. A publish that fails is logged
(`storage`, `stored-files.publish-failed`) and leaves the call's result alone.

Before, the uploads' gate time grew with the recording (each minute adds 11 records, 5,584 by minute 120); after, it is
about a millisecond a minute. The CPU and footprint rows move within the shared machine's noise (the before run's
last ten minutes ran under other agents' load); the per-holder gate time is the direct measure. After the fix, the
largest holder is the outbox pump's claim: 402-524 ms of every minute, about 60% of the gate (#1108, #1114).

The whole audit (every hot-path operation at both sizes, who calls it, what its cost grows with, and its issue) is
`docs/audits/2026-10-24-whole-store-cost-audit.md`.

## Real-size budgets in CI

`RealSizeBudgetTests` runs in the correctness job's release build: 18 operations metered at both sizes must cost about
the same on the larger store (allocations and instructions within 1.5x) and stay under a ceiling at the phone's size.
The ones that still grow carry their own issues as known issues (#1103 discard, #1104 eviction and storage snapshot,
#1106 refusal supersessions, #1108 the outbox pump's claim, #1111 per-call schema work), so a fix shows up as the known
issue no longer happening. `INSTANT_REAL_SIZE_REPORT=1` prints the whole report; `INSTANT_REAL_SIZE_RECORDING_MINUTES`
runs a recording's minutes at the phone's size. Tests only: no library code.

## What stays the same

No schema, migration or public API change. An open observation of the device's files receives every upload, delete,
download and eviction as before. An upload, read or delete no longer fails, and an upload no longer deletes its stored
file, when another record cannot be read.
