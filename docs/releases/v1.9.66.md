## Release focus

**The store's newest transaction time is kept, not walked** (#1748; red `b446b586`, green `9722630e`, comment
`e4990383`).

- A full-rebase server apply stamps each pending write it replays one past the store's newest fact, so it read the
  newest once per write, and that read walked every fact: a frame that replays P pending writes over N facts cost
  P × N. On 2026-11-04 iOS's CPU Monitor killed an app on that path ("48 seconds cpu time over 60 seconds (81% cpu
  average), exceeding limit of 80% cpu over 60 seconds", the app not frontmost), with 5 of the report's 6 samples in
  the walk; the same app's CPU reports show the walk growing with its unsent writes (10 of 32 samples at 01:57, 13 of
  22 at 02:55, 29 of 38 at 10:39).
- `TripleIndexes` keeps how many facts carry each transaction time in sorted runs (`TransactionTimeCounts`): the newest
  is the last entry, and when the last fact at the newest time leaves (a peeled overlay, a removed deferred value, a
  retraction) the next newest is the entry before it. Each change costs O(log D) for D distinct times; a write prepared
  on a copy of the indexes copies the array of runs and the run it changes.
- Kept where the fact count is kept, and where a cardinality-one value replaces another; counted again on decode and
  on a derived-index rebuild. Not encoded, compared or hashed: it is derived from the facts.

Measured (`InstantStoreWriteScalingTests.replayingPendingWritesDoesNotWalkTheStoreForTheNewestTime`, a debug build on
a laptop at load ~300, build.sh job `1748-redgreen`):

| Measure | Before (`b446b586`) | After (`9722630e`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 300 replayed writes over 2,000 facts (best of 3) | 0.496 s | not printed: the test prints only when it fails |
| 300 replayed writes over 64,000 facts (best of 3) | 17.998 s | within the test's bound: under 4× the 2,000-fact time, or under 20 ms |
| the whole test, setup included | 68.920 s | 0.368 s |

Not measured: the release-mode server-apply cost harness (`INSTANT_SERVER_APPLY_COST=1`): `swift test -c release` does
not link the package's `todos-v3` demo on the laptop's macOS 26.7 (an undefined SwiftUI symbol), before and after
alike; and the effect on a device, until a build with this version runs there.
