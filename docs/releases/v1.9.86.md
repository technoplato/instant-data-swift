## Before you upgrade

No change on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS or watchOS. On tvOS the default live store moves from
`Library/Application Support/InstantSwiftData/apps/` to `Library/Caches/InstantSwiftData/apps/`, and the default
diagnostics log from `Library/Logs/InstantSwiftData/` to `Library/Caches/Logs/InstantSwiftData/`. No tvOS app could
create the old folders on a device, so there is nothing to move. The system may purge Caches when the device is low on
space; the store is then rebuilt from the server.

## Release focus

**An Apple TV app opens** (#2330; red and green below).

- Why: on an Apple TV, a consuming app's launch failed with `NSCocoaErrorDomain` 513, POSIX 1 "Operation not
  permitted", creating `Library/Application Support/InstantSwiftData/apps`. tvOS lets an app write only its Caches and
  tmp folders; the tvOS simulator does not enforce it, so simulator runs never showed it.
- Now: `InstantStoragePlatform` (in `InstantSwiftDataCore`) names the platform and its writable folders;
  `defaultInstantSwiftDataPersistenceURL(appID:context:uniqueID:platform:)` and
  `InstantDiagnostics.defaultLogFileURL(processName:platform:)` take it, defaulting to `.current`.
